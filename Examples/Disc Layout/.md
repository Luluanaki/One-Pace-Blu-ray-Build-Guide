# Notes

* This disc structure is purely just for reference and what worked for me. Feel free to do what works for you.

* I really wanted to fit Sabaody Archipelago in with the rest of the Summit War saga but I just couldn't fit it without going below 4 mbps. I think disc 7 turned out to be my largest disc, but it works out because an entire disc for Thriller Bark would have been sad anyways and it ends on a good cliffhanger.

* If you don't care about saga structure and just want as few discs as possible this can definitely be compressed.
